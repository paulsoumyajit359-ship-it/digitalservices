Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 August 2026

Digital Service respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide while using our website and digital service facilities.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we may collect, why we collect it, how it may be used, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, submit an application or contact us, we may collect information such as:

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • WhatsApp number, where provided
  • Address and other information supplied in an application
  • Service requirements and application details
  • Documents uploaded by you for a requested service
  • Account and authentication information

2. How We Use Your Information

Information provided by customers may be used for purposes including:

  • Creating and managing your customer account
  • Processing your requested service
  • Reviewing application information and documents
  • Communicating with you regarding your application
  • Providing application status updates
  • Responding to customer support requests
  • Improving our website and service experience
  • Preventing misuse, fraud or unauthorized activity

3. Documents and Sensitive Information

Certain services may require identity, address, tax, business or other supporting documents. You should upload only documents that are necessary for the service you are requesting.

Please do not upload documents that are unrelated to your application or share passwords, OTPs, banking PINs or other authentication credentials through the website.

4. Storage and Service Providers

Information submitted through the website may be processed and stored using third-party technology and hosting services used to operate Digital Service.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or destruction. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell your personal information.

Information may be shared where reasonably necessary to process the requested service, operate the website, provide technical services, comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, or protect the rights and security of customers and the service.

6. Customer Responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that the information and documents submitted by you are accurate, complete and lawfully provided.

Do not submit another person's personal information or documents without appropriate authorization.

7. Cookies and Website Technology

The website may use browser storage, cookies or similar technologies where required for authentication, security, functionality and improving the user experience.

8. Account Security

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials confidential and should immediately contact us if they believe their account has been accessed without authorization.

Digital Service will not ask customers to disclose passwords, OTPs, PINs or other confidential authentication credentials.

9. Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to government portals, payment providers or other third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and customers should review those policies before submitting information to them.

10. Data Retention

We may retain application and account information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the requested services, maintain business records, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations or protect legitimate interests.

11. Your Requests

Subject to applicable law and operational requirements, you may contact us regarding access, correction or deletion of personal information associated with your account.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

13. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact Digital Service through the contact details provided on our website.
